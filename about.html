What we stand for

Four principles, held stubbornly.

Select a principle to see how it shows up in the product.

Radical transparency

Every charge on a NovaTrade contract note is itemised — brokerage, exchange fees, and statutory levies are never bundled into a spread.

We publish monthly execution-quality statistics, including price improvement and venue distribution, so clients can verify our claims rather than trust them.

Security as default

Hardware-key 2FA is available on every tier, not reserved for premium accounts. Client securities sit in segregated custody, entirely separate from company funds.

Internal access to production systems is time-boxed, peer-approved, and fully audit-logged.

Obsessive performance

Our matching gateway is colocated with primary venues, and the order path is measured end to end on every single fill.

A latency regression is treated with the same severity as an outage — if the median fill slips past 25ms, we roll back.

Client alignment

We do not sell order flow. Our revenue comes from a flat, visible fee, which means our incentive is your execution quality, not your turnover.

No inactivity fees, no withdrawal fees, and no penalty for closing an account.
